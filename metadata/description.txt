Pitchtape is a sideline sheet for coaches and parents. Tap Goal, Foul, Card, or Sub onto the running clock on the ON-AIR board. This is not a betting board.

• ON-AIR console with live clock, Home and Away score, call pad, and take tape
• Start the clock with Home, Away, and a Pitch, Ice, or Court template
• Goal, Foul, Card, and Sub write a Call on the open tape
• Undo peels the last Call on the open period
• Whistle parks the period and opens the next empty tape
• Matches starts or resumes a fixture
• Insights lists parked tapes and Export parked tapes as PDF
• Settings: Sport, Contact us, Re-run onboarding, Reset all data
• First-run walkthrough with Continue, Skip, and Start the clock
• Periods and calls stay on this device

Sport templates only label the next kickoff. Odds and money are not in the product.
